# Product Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Discovery and foundations
Architecture decisions, UX flows, threat model, legal/privacy requirements, environments, CI/CD and design system.

## Phase 1 — Identity and roles
Authentication, sessions, role selection, rider onboarding, RBAC foundation.

## Phase 2 — Maps and rider
Location search, Google Maps, saved places, ride-purpose selection and quote UX.

## Phase 3 — Provider onboarding
Driver and Guide three-step verification, documents, live verification and admin review.

## Phase 4 — Booking and dispatch
Ride lifecycle, pricing, Driver dispatch, acceptance/rejection and cancellation.

## Phase 5 — Guide marketplace
Pickup/destination search, filters, guide assignment and combined service.

## Phase 6 — Realtime
WebSockets, location updates, event reconciliation, push notifications.

## Phase 7 — Payments
Razorpay integration, cash, server verification, webhooks, refunds and earnings.

## Phase 8 — Safety
Safety Center, SOS, heartbeat, trusted contacts, trip sharing and emergency operations.

## Phase 9 — Recovery
Interrupted ride, replacement dispatch, fare ledger and recovery workflows.

## Phase 10 — Admin
Operations, verification, emergency command center, finance, notifications, promotions, support and analytics.

## Phase 11 — Hardening
Load tests, security review, privacy review, battery/location review, observability, disaster recovery.

## Phase 12 — Production
Pilot city/region, operational playbooks, staged rollout, incident response and post-launch optimization.

## Definition of done for every phase
Database/model → backend/API → Android → Admin where applicable → permissions → tests → observability → security review → documentation.
## Localization roadmap
Build the language catalog, account preference, Android localization, Admin translation management and localized notifications from the foundation phases instead of retrofitting multilingual support later.
